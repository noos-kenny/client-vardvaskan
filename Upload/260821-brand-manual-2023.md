## 1.0 Introduction

This is a visual guide for everyone working with Vårdväskan's visual identity — from digital experiences to physical communication.

Vårdväskan's new identity was created to strengthen the brand's market position through a stringent and distinct brand that visualizes the brand values — whether in the digital experience or meeting Vårdväskan in a physical environment. **The visual expression is aspirational, energetic and credible.**

Vårdväskan enables personality, pride and professionalism to be expressed within healthcare, and that shows in every part of the identity.

## 2.0 Colors

**Vårdväskan is a pink company**, and it is important that the pink color gets enough space in the visual communication. The palette is modern and strong and can be combined in different ways depending on surface and context.

To give the primary pink room across many levels of communication there is a lighter and a darker variant that can work as background or accent colors. Beyond the pink primary palette there are three complementary colors and several shades of black.

## 2.1 Color combinations

The primary rule for color combinations is **contrast** — choose the combination that creates the best contrast against the background or image.

More shades of the primary pink exist for digital applications and backgrounds, for easier use of the primary pink while building brand recognition. They can be used in several ways, but **Vårdväskan as a whole should be perceived as a pink company**.

Approved text-color combinations: primary pink + white text; light pink + primary pink text; white + primary pink text; image + primary pink text; red + white text; purple + primary pink text; blue + primary pink text; image + white text.

## 3.0 Logotype

The logotype is clean and modern, based on the graphic symbol. The symbol together with the wordmark forms the logotype — the most important brand component. Place the logotype with great respect for space and contrast; used primarily in **white, primary pink or black**.

In some contexts the symbol can be used as a standalone graphic element, e.g. on tote bags, boxes or other graphic material communicated together with the rest of the brand.

**Color4Care:** for the international markets the Color4Care logotype is used. The same rules for color and placement apply as for Vårdväskan.

Approved logotype color combinations: primary pink + white logo; white + black logo; light pink + primary pink logo; image + primary pink logo; white + primary pink logo; image + white logo. The color that shows best against the background should always be used; where possible, use the same text color as logo color.

## 3.1 Logotype placement

- Primary placement: centered. Secondary: centered at the bottom edge, or left-aligned toward the bottom-left corner.
- A: the logotype's height is used as the minimum clear space between logotype and edge.
- B: the logotype's height plus the icon's width is used as minimum clear space when placed toward a corner.
- Where the logotype is larger than usual, this distance may need to be reduced.
- The logotype should never be smaller than 3 cm (width).

## 4.0 Typography

Vårdväskan's primary typeface is **Graphik** — legible, versatile, and reinforcing the feeling of a modern, fast-moving company.

- Graphik is used in the weights **Bold, Medium and Regular**.
- Headlines can be written in ALL CAPS for extra visual punch; for longer headlines or a more toned-down expression, use sentence case.
- The Medium weight is used for sub-headings and over-headings but should be avoided in longer texts.
- For a clean, modern expression, headlines and body text should have sufficiently large contrast in size: keep headlines large and clean, body text small but readable.

## 5.0 Photography

Vårdväskan's photographic style is **modern, pared-back and human**. Whether showing people or products the feeling should be the same. In an aspirational way, natural people of different genders, ages and appearances are shown wearing the clothes and products. People can be in studio or on location; images are bright, clean and professional.

**Product photography:** products can be shot flat in studio or with human presence. They should be shot on a light-grey background, well lit, for a bright and clean expression — also giving the best color reproduction and detail.

An alternative to the clean product images is colorful, conceptual product images shot from above in studio, following a clear color theme and showing a breadth of products in a single image. The complementary colors can be used for this.

## 6.0 Graphic elements

Graphic elements can be used across the identity — to communicate offers, news or price. Using the primary pink as much as possible in these builds extra brand recognition without taking over completely. (Examples shown, e.g. "NYHET!", "ERBJUDANDE".)

**Icons:** for a unified expression, only icons from the icon library are used. Contact the responsible person for access.

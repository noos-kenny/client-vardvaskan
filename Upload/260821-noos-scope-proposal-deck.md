## 02. NOOS x Vårdväskan

### Brief and background — Commercial insights and creative alignment (NOOS's understanding of scope)

Vårdväskan grew by 15% in 2025, reaching 102 MSEK in revenue. To reach positive EBITDA, the company has set a revenue target of 114 MSEK for 2026.

Achieving this requires scaling growth in the categories that drive both margin and brand value, where the current limiting factor is content and creative production. This limitation is most evident in the categories that are strategically most important from a margin and brand perspective. The prioritized categories are: **work shoes, compression socks and work accessories**.

These categories combine high margins, a growing share of private label products and strong long-term growth potential, but they are currently underleveraged in marketing due to limited scalable, category-specific content.

The objective is to build a structured full-funnel content and creative approach that explains product value, builds demand earlier in the customer journey and enables continuous testing and optimization across channels.

### Strategic Approach for Vårdväskan: Combining three key areas (NOOS proposal)

**01 Commercial Insights** — Create a clear understanding of who buys, why they buy, and how growth is created within work shoes, compression socks and accessories.

- Define purchase journeys, target audiences and sub-segments per category
- Analyse customer behaviour and repeat purchase dynamics
- Clarify purchase triggers, motivations, and barriers per category
- Identify which categories drive new customer acquisition
- Define CRM strategy for cross- and upsell between categories
- Identify HERO products for acquisition vs upsell for existing customers
- Assess alignment between commercial strategy, marketing and CRM

Delivery: Clear prioritisation of audiences, journeys and motivational drivers. A commercial foundation for content and CRM activation.

**02 Creative Audit & Strategy** — Translate commercial insights into category-specific creative strategies that drive sales.

- Audit existing content vs target audiences and funnel stages
- Identify content gaps limiting scale and performance
- Define creative strategy per category and persona
- Develop concrete ad concepts with clear messages and hooks

Delivery: Actionable creative strategy and ad concepts per category, ready for production and testing.

**03 Creative Activation** — Ensure content is activated as a commercial system, not isolated assets, to maximise new customer acquisition and customer retention (LTV).

- Review how content is currently activated across Meta, Google and CRM
- Define how content should drive: new customer acquisition via paid media; upsell and repeat purchase via CRM and retargeting
- Clarify roles for always-on, campaign and lifecycle activation

Delivery: A clear activation framework linking content to acquisition, upsell and revenue growth.

### Insights turn into a creative strategy (NOOS methodology example — generic trail-running example, not Vårdväskan-specific)

Three levels: 01 Audience (who the customers are), 02 Full funnel mastery (what stage they are in), 03 Creative diversification (what creative concepts and angles drive attention, consideration and conversion).

Example chain: Audience Segment (trail runners / outdoor enthusiasts 25–45) → Purchase trigger (preparing for a trail run, exploring new terrain) → Motivation (run further in nature, feel confident on technical terrain) → Objections (grip concerns, durability, worth the premium price). Funnel: Attention (grab attention, stop the scroll) → Consideration (problem, solution and motivation) → Conversion (objections, friction and conversion techniques). From creative situations to creative concepts (e.g., POV trail run showing grip on rocky terrain) and creative angles ("Run beyond the road" / "Built for technical terrain").

### Hook testing framework (NOOS methodology — generic examples)

Different types of hooks engage different audience segments. Hook testing framework based on human behaviour research:

- **Question** — "Can your running shoes handle this terrain?"
- **Curiosity** — "This trail destroyed my last running shoes."
- **Pain Point** — "Most running shoes fail on trails like this."
- **Performance** — "Built for the toughest terrain."
- **Identity** — "For runners who leave the road behind."
- **Comparison** — "Road shoes vs trail shoes on real terrain."
- **Social proof** — "Why trail runners are switching to this shoe."
- **Demonstration** — "Watch the grip on this rocky descent."
- **Transformation** — "From slipping on trails to running with confidence."
- **Speed** — "Fast enough for the road. Tough enough for the trail."

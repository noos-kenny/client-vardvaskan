## Project Scope and Description

**Growth Retainer**

NOOS will manage the Client's monthly advertising accounts, with the goal of optimizing and improving performance to increase brand awareness, revenue, and profit.

- Platforms: Google, Bing, Meta, Snapchat, TikTok, LinkedIn
- Markets: SE, NO, FI, DK, DE

**Campaign Management & Optimization**

- Activating and continuously optimizing new campaigns based on set and agreed targets
- Developing Shopping/Performance Max campaigns
- Re-allocating budgets between campaigns, ad groups, and listing groups to achieve the best possible return on investment (ROI) and growth
- Developing catalogue campaigns (DPA campaigns)
- Ongoing advertisement optimization, including bid strategies, keyword research, negative keywords, audiences, ad copy, and creatives

**Audience Targeting**

- Identifying and testing new target audiences to expand reach and engagement

**Reporting**

- Monthly meetings to review campaign performance, discuss insights, and plan future activities
- Performance reporting on a weekly or bi-weekly basis
- Performance dashboard for reporting

**Creative Insights & Strategy**

**Creative Production**

- Ownership of the Smartly + Sheet solution
- Support with a creative workflow utilizing AI
- Potential support during peak season

**Assortment & Price Strategy**

- Aligning the product and creative strategy
- Potential support with price strategy on a project basis

**Evaluation of the new top funnel strategy.**

**Support with organic search on a project basis.**

## Client general obligations

The Client shall provide the marketing material, data, information and other necessary details required for the execution of the assignment. The Client shall approve marketing materials in advance before external publication.

The Client shall provide a representative who represents the Client in matters related to the assignment and has decision-making authority in matters concerning the assignment.

The Client shall retain full ownership of all accounts, data, creative assets, landing pages, and other materials related to the assignment.

## NOOS general obligations

NOOS commits to providing specialist(s) at the Client's disposal ("the Resource") for the duration of the agreement.

The Resource's working hours for performing the assignment are specified in "Cost Breakdown". The Resource assigned to perform the assignment shall have appropriate training and experience to professionally carry out the assignment in accordance with the Agreement.

NOOS commits to observing professional ethics and adhering to industry practices, as well as complying with applicable laws, regulations, guidelines and practices along with the instructions provided by the Client.

Throughout the execution of the assignment, NOOS shall keep the results continuously accessible to the Client. Upon the Client's request, NOOS shall provide reports on the results during the assignments.

## Confidentiality & Information

Each party commits to not disclose or otherwise make available any confidential information to any third party without the written consent of the other party. Confidential information, for the purposes of this Agreement, includes the commercial terms in this Agreement and any other information received by either party in connection with this Agreement and originating from the other party, regardless of how the receiving party acquired the information.

Confidential information does not include:

a) Information that was already publicly known when disclosed under this Agreement

b) Information that the party has developed or obtained entirely independently of the information received from the other party

c) Information that is lawfully obtained by the receiving party from a third party and does not violate this confidentiality commitment

NOOS agrees to ensure that employees, consultants, subconsultants and owners of NOOS do not disclose confidential information to third parties.

## Terms and Conditions

Monthly invoicing in accordance with specifications under "Cost Breakdown", in arrears with 30 days payment terms. All prices specified are excluding VAT.

Price adjustment according to the Agreement from 1/1 attributable to general cost changes. Calculated based on Statistics Sweden's Labor Cost Index (SNI, 2007), officials, column M (companies in law, economics, science and technology).

This Agreement is valid from the moment the parties sign the Agreement and is in effect indefinitely. The mutual notice period shall be three full calendar months. In practical terms, if either party provides notice of termination on a specific date, the agreement will remain in effect until the last day of the third full calendar month following the date of notice.

Once the start date is agreed upon and confirmed in writing for related services, fees and terms of conditions shall be governed by and in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

Each party has the right to terminate this Agreement immediately or at a time determined by the party if the other party:

a) materially breaches its obligations under this Agreement and despite written notice, does not remedy the breach within thirty (30) days; or

b) becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation, becomes the subject of a bankruptcy application, corporate reorganization, or seizure, or is otherwise deemed to be in financial distress.

Termination must be communicated in writing by the terminating party. The other party must confirm the termination in writing. For the purpose of this agreement, email communication shall be considered a valid form of written communication.

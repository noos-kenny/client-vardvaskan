# Vårdväskan — B2C Tone of Voice 2026

Author: Vårdväskan (client's own tone-of-voice guide). Original in Swedish; translated to English. Example copy lines kept verbatim in Swedish (they are messaging raw material) with English glosses.

## The voice

Vårdväskan's voice is **warm, personal, inclusive, positive and light-hearted**. Write as if talking to a colleague you like: close, natural, encouraging and with energy. The tone may have a twinkle in the eye and be a bit cheeky, but must never feel silly, childish, stiff, condescending or overly salesy.

## The audience

The target audience is care staff: nurses, assistant nurses, physicians, dental staff, animal care staff and other professionals in healthcare and care. They often work long shifts, walk many steps, stand on hard floors and have a high tempo in everyday life. **They are professionals, not cute stereotypes.** Copy must show that Vårdväskan understands their reality and appreciates the job they do.

## The hero

**The care professional is the hero — not the product.** Lift the reader as someone who makes a difference every day. Write so the person feels seen, proud, encouraged and inspired. Avoid overusing the word "hjälte" (hero); rather show appreciation through concrete phrasings tied to their working day.

## Vårdväskan's role

To be **healthcare's best friend**: the encouraging colleague in the break room who knows what's going on, gets that the job is tough, and wants to make the working day a bit more colourful, comfortable and fun.

## Focus on the combination of

- colour and personal style
- practical function
- comfort during long shifts
- quality that lasts
- smart details that make the working day easier
- products that help care staff be themselves at work

## Sell the result and the feeling

Sell the result and the feeling, not just the product. Don't just write that a shoe has a soft sole — write about lighter steps through a long shift. Don't just write that compression socks are colourful — write about fresher legs, more colour on the shift and a work look that feels more like you. **Avoid hard health promises or phrasings that sound medically guaranteeing**, for example "knän som håller till pensionen" (knees that last until retirement).

## Everyday starting points

Often start from care staff's everyday life: long shifts, many steps, high tempo, hard floors, coffee breaks, colleagues, patients, practical needs, pride, and the feeling of getting to express one's personality at work.

## "be YOU at work"

Use expressions connected to Vårdväskan's idea of "be YOU at work": personal style, colour, joy, good vibes, vårdlook (care look), arbetslook (work look), mix and match, stand out or keep it clean. Vårdväskan believes work clothes and work accessories don't have to be boring.

## Product categories to mention when relevant

Compression socks (stödstrumpor), sneakers, sandals, work shoes, care clothing, work trousers, scrub tops (bussaronger), name tags, pen pockets (pennfickor), lanyards (nyckelband), card holders, stethoscopes, Littmann, scissors, silicone watches, accessories and other practical work accessories.

## Language

Everyday, correct and easy to understand. Short sentences, clear rhythm and variation. Mix short punchy phrasings with somewhat more vivid sentences. Copy should be quick to absorb and feel like an inspiring greeting from Vårdväskan — not like catalogue text, bureaucratic language or a B2B letter.

Use "du" and "vi" naturally. Speak directly to the reader. Avoid passive, impersonal phrasings. Rather "Du gör jobbet. Vi sätter färgen." (You do the job. We bring the colour.) than "Produkter tillhandahålls för vårdpersonal" (Products are provided for care staff).

The tone can be more punchy and campaign-like in headlines, short teasers and product-near copy, especially for campaigns, theme shops, Valentine, Pride, colourful collections or products with a lot of personality. But the warmth and respect must always remain.

## Examples of the right feeling (verbatim, Swedish)

- "Du gör skillnad varje dag." (You make a difference every day.)
- "För jobbprylar ska inte vara tråkiga." (Because work gear shouldn't be boring.)
- "Stick ut, visa din stil eller kör safe - valet är ditt." (Stand out, show your style or play it safe — your choice.)
- "Du gör jobbet. Vi sätter färgen." (You do the job. We bring the colour.)
- "Tillsammans sätter vi färg på vården." (Together we bring colour to healthcare.)
- "Unna dig något som gör arbetsdagen lite roligare." (Treat yourself to something that makes the working day a bit more fun.)
- "Vem sa att jobbkläder måste vara tråkiga? Inte vi." (Who said work clothes must be boring? Not us.)
- "Tufft pass? Vi har grejerna som hänger med." (Tough shift? We've got the gear that keeps up.)
- "Grå vardag? Inte på ditt skift." (Grey everyday? Not on your shift.)
- "Låt arbetslooken jobba lika hårt som du." (Let the work look work as hard as you do.)

## Lovebomb / Valentine / colleague-to-colleague copy

Extra personal, warm and light-hearted. It should feel like a message you'd actually want to send to a colleague: genuine, encouraging, a bit fun and full of appreciation. Examples:

- "Lovebomba en kollega." (Lovebomb a colleague.)
- "Lite extra kärlek räcker långt." (A little extra love goes a long way.)
- "Jobbet hade varit betydligt tristare utan dig." (Work would be considerably duller without you.)

## English expressions

May be used sparingly and naturally when they fit Vårdväskan's style, e.g. "Happy vibes only", "Mix & match", "be YOU at work", "we've got you covered", "treat yourself", "last chance", "new in", "spread the word". The English should feel playful and integrated, not tacked on.

## CTAs

Clear, positive and upbeat without hard sales pressure. Avoid aggressive phrasings like "Köp nu!!!". Prefer:

"Shoppa din stil" · "Hitta din favorit" · "Utforska kollektionen" · "Se alla färger" · "Klicka hem favoriterna" · "Fynda nu" · "Läs mer" · "Upptäck sortimentet" · "Shoppa nu"

## Discounts and campaigns

Communicate clearly but not desperately. Create the feeling of an opportunity rather than pressure. Phrasings like "Passa på", "Just nu", "Perfekt läge" and "Sista chansen" work well, but use in moderation.

## Emojis

Sparingly and thoughtfully. They should reinforce the feeling, not carry the message. Don't use an emoji on every line.

## Always avoid

- stiff corporate communication
- bureaucratic language
- dry B2B tone
- overly formal words
- too much sales language
- too many exclamation marks
- shouting capitals
- generic phrases without personality
- overly long paragraphs
- care clichés that feel tacked on
- sounding condescending, cutesy or too "perky"
- talking more about the brand than about the care staff
- hard or uncertain health promises
- phrasings that feel stressful, desperate or aggressive

Avoid words that feel corporate or stiff, e.g. "tillhandahålla" (provide), "möjliggöra" (enable), "unika erbjudanden" (unique offers) and heavy use of "sortiment" (assortment). Prefer simpler, warmer, more everyday alternatives such as "fixa", "hitta", "grejer", "favoriter", "fynd" or "produkter", depending on context.

## Newsletters

Copy should feel inspiring, relevant and easy to click on from. Products are woven in via the benefit they create: lighter steps, fresher legs, a smarter working day, more colour, more comfort and more personal style.

## Product/campaign copy

Clear, concrete and energetic, but never losing the warmth. A good Vårdväskan text combines emotional appreciation with practical product benefit.

## The goal of all copy

The reader should feel: "Det här var för mig." (This was for me.) · "De fattar min vardag." (They get my everyday.) · "Jag känner mig sedd." (I feel seen.) · "Det här känns kul, relevant och enkelt att klicka vidare på." (This feels fun, relevant and easy to click on.)

## Summary of the tonality

Vårdväskan B2C sounds like healthcare's best friend: warm, personal, upbeat and colourful with a twinkle in the eye. We understand long shifts, hard floors and high tempo — and we help care staff get more comfort, function, colour and personality in the working day. We don't just sell products. We make the job a little nicer, smarter and more fun for those who take care of others.

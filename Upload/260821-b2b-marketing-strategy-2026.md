## Target audience and segments (B2B)

We will not focus on the large municipal and state procurement tenders. Our key segments are:

- **Small clinics** — businesses with 1–10 employees who want a simple process for ordering their workwear without minimum-quantity requirements.
- **Medium-sized clinics** — an optimal customer is companies like Kry, Doktor.se, Oral Care, Praktikertjänst etc. Private operators established across large parts of the country.
- **Professional groups** — target audiences can also be divided into professional categories/industries.

## Positioning and messaging (B2B)

**Why should the customer choose us?**

- **Who?** Small to medium-sized clinics within healthcare; purchasers; medical secretaries; nurses; assistant nurses.
- **Why?** Favourable loyalty program (discounts); simplifies order placement; everything in one place for the whole business; personal service / key account; try before buy — Provlådan (the sample box); shoe allowance solution (skobidrag); customized assortment; mix n match.
- **What?** Professional products; one-stop-shop; wide assortment; company account (företagskonto); personal service; Provlådan; skobidrag + the skobidrag solution.

Service pillars: FÖRETAGSKONTO (company account) · BRETT UTBUD (wide assortment) · NAMNSKYLTAR (name tags) · PROVLÅDAN (sample box) · PERSONLIG SERVICE (personal service) · TRYCK & BRODYR (print & embroidery).

**Positioning statement:** "We make purchasing simple — and staff prouder" ("Vi gör inköpen enkla – och personalen stoltare"). Choosing Vårdväskan Företag is more than just purchasing workwear. It is an investment in the team's wellbeing and makes your everyday more efficient. We combine the Nordics' widest assortment and smooth business services with a genuine understanding of healthcare's reality. With services like Provlådan (risk-free on-site trials) and automated handling of skobidrag, we eliminate cumbersome administration. At the same time, your staff get clothes and equipment that strengthen professional pride, fit comfortably and withstand tough conditions.

Quote: "Låt oss sköta logistiken med smarta företagstjänster, så kan ni fokusera på vården – klädda i branschens bästa och skönaste plagg." ("Let us handle the logistics with smart business services, so you can focus on care — dressed in the industry's best and most comfortable garments.")

**Why healthcare chooses us:**

- **Provlådan — risk-free & convenient:** move the fitting room to the break room. Order a free sample box and let staff test sizes and models in peace. Free shipping, free returns, no purchase obligation.
- **Företagskonto — control & profitability:** better standard prices directly, volume discount that grows with purchases; full overview of who orders; consolidated invoicing that helps the finance department.
- **Skobidrag — zero administration:** stop chasing receipts. We automate the whole flow with unique codes for staff. They order their favourite shoes themselves; you get one consolidated monthly invoice.
- **Profilering — strengthen team spirit:** put your mark on the department with customized name tags based on your branding; smooth handling of print and embroidery so workwear reflects your identity and professionalism.

**Message / tagline (NOTE: marked "ej spikat" — not finalized):**

- "Vårdväskan Företag - Enklare inköp och stoltare vårdpersonal" (Simpler purchasing and prouder care staff)
- "Vårdväskan Företag - Arbetskläder, utrustning och namnskyltar för ert team" (Workwear, equipment and name tags for your team)

## Marketing (B2B)

**Where should we be visible and where can we reach our customers?**

Digital: LinkedIn, Meta, YouTube, email marketing, content marketing (blog, guides & testimonials), social proof (testimonials), SEO, Google Ads & display advertising.

Physical: industry fairs and conferences (pop-up shop), physical meetings & product demonstrations, catalogues & printed marketing, industry press.

| Channel | Purpose | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta (Instagram/FB) | Awareness & recognition | Visual ads towards staff/managers. Focus on pain points and services. |
| LinkedIn | Lead gen & authority | Targeted ads towards HR, operations managers and purchasers. Focus on "admin headache" (skobidrag), reference cases (Kry), simple workwear trials (Provlådan) etc. |
| Google (Ads/SEO) | Capture purchase intent | Be visible on search terms like "arbetskläder vård", "skobidrag personal", "bussarong med tryck" etc. |
| Email (CRM) | Retention & upsell | Automated flows. Seasonal purchase reminders, Provlådan follow-up, anti-churn etc. |
| Catalogue/Physical | Top of mind | Mailings to break rooms and purchasers. Provlådan acts as a physical salesperson on site. |

**Digital activations:**

- Content marketing: valuable content (blog posts, guides) targeting businesses — solving pain points, educating, building recognition.
- SEO and SEM: optimize for industry-specific search terms.
- Social media: build LinkedIn presence focused on insights and solutions for care staff needs; include customer cases and experiences from existing collaborations.
- Customer surveys: regularly request customer feedback to improve the offering, assortment and services.
- Social proof / testimonials: content describing how we have helped other care providers; satisfied customers' stories.
- Digital catalogue: use the physical catalogue as a marketing tool digitally too.
- Always on: always have rolling paid social ads with brand messages to reach potential audiences and build audiences.

**How do we win the customer?** Favourable loyalty program (discounts); simplified ordering; everything in one place for the whole business; personal service / key account; try before buy with Provlådan; skobidrag solution; customized assortment (total solution); mix n match across brands (wide assortment).

## Campaigns and seasons (B2B) — touchpoints during the year

- **Q1:** Spring catalogue mailing #1; "Are you ready for the new year?" (equip the team)
- **Q2:** Spring catalogue mailing #2; prep for summer & temporary staff
- **Q3:** Autumn catalogue #1; Back to work (do you have everything you need?)
- **Q4:** Autumn catalogue mailing #2; Swedental trade fair; equip for winter (outdoor assortment); burn budget (end of year)

## Lead generation and CRM (B2B)

- **Hubspot:** maximize the possibilities and set a clear strategy for how to work with it based on the version we have access to.
- **Landing pages and forms:** dedicated B2B landing pages with forms to easily order samples (Provlådan), request quotes, book meetings with KAM/KT, or request contact.
- **NL journeys:** segment B2B customers in Mailchimp and create automated email sends and journeys. Review content mix and plan.
- **Follow-ups & Leadfeeder:** follow up all leads from fairs, social media and Leadfeeder to increase conversions.
- **Proactive customer service:** proactive customer service team contacting customers to offer help or suggest new products/services.

## Our customers B2C

**Customers:**

- More than 90% of our customers are women.
- Nurses (sjuksköterskor) and assistant nurses (undersköterskor) are the two main professions, representing more than 85% of our customers in the B2C segment.
- The third biggest customer group is physicians (läkare), representing just under 10%.

**Categories & products:**

- Only minor category variations within the customer segment (e.g. assistant nurses do not buy equipment).
- Category variations are seen across markets (e.g. FI is big on clothing).
- Similar colour schemes across all markets, where black, navy and white dominate on all markets.

**Purchase patterns:**

- New and returning customers have similar purchase patterns.
- Exceptions: compression socks are more represented among returning customers, and Littmann is a one-time purchase.

## Positioning & messaging (B2C)

**Why should the customer choose us?**

- **Who?** Nurses, assistant nurses, medical secretaries, physicians, veterinarians, dentists, and more.
- **Why?** Make the working day easier; personal expression; professional pride; be empowered / independent / secure; belonging; simple consolidated purchases.
- **What?** Professional products; one-stop-shop; wide assortment; community; affordable; inspiration.

**Positioning statement:** "Vi ger din arbetsdag färg och din kropp styrkan att orka" ("We give your working day colour and your body the strength to endure"). Choosing Vårdväskan is more than just updating your equipment. It is an investment in your own health and a way to bring your personal style to work. We combine the Nordics' widest assortment for care professionals with genuine respect for the job you do. We know that healthcare floors are hard and the shifts long. That is why we offer a unique mix of ergonomics and joy — from shoes that save your back and feet, to colourful details that spread smiles in the corridor. We make it easy to find products that both maximize your comfort and strengthen your professional pride.

Quote: "Vi tar hand om dig, så att du orkar ta hand om andra, med utrustning som både känns bra och ser bra ut." ("We take care of you, so you can keep taking care of others, with equipment that both feels good and looks good.")

**Why healthcare chooses us:**

- **"Lite mindre vitt. Mycket mer du."** (A little less white. Much more you.) Healthcare's dress code often erases the individual. We give you the tools to take back your personality, so you can be you at work too.
- **"För dig som står upp för andra."** (For you who stand up for others.) We understand the person behind the professional role. We don't just sell shoes, but equipment that protects your body and helps you last the whole career, not just the whole shift.
- **"Tillsammans sätter vi färg på vården."** (Together we bring colour to healthcare.) We are not an external supplier, we are a movement. Together with you we make a sterile environment warmer, more fun and more human — for both staff and patients.
- **"Din skönaste kollega."** (Your most comfortable/nicest colleague.) Someone you can trust, with products that make a real difference. Products that feel like a hug for your feet. Compression socks that let your legs last long shifts. A partner in the everyday who makes sure blisters, tired legs or aching backs never become an obstacle to your competence.
- **"Allt för ditt skift. Utan krångel."** (Everything for your shift. No hassle.) Your time is precious, so we have gathered everything from scissors and watches to shoes and compression socks under one roof. Products tested for healthcare's reality, so you don't have to gamble.

**Message / brand tagline / sign-off:**

- Short version: "Vårdväskan - Tillsammans sätter vi färg på vården"
- Alternative version: "För dig som står upp för andra → Tillsammans sätter vi färg på vården."

## Tonality (B2C)

Tone: **we are the encouraging colleague in the break room — warm, quick and on your side.**

1. **We are one of you.** We don't talk *to* healthcare, we talk *with* it. No bureaucratic words. We talk about "hard floors", "coffee stains" and "12-hour shifts". We show that we get it.
2. **We are a splash of colour in an otherwise sterile environment.** In a world of clinical white and grey, we are the dopamine kick. We dare to be playful and a bit cheeky, use exclamation marks, emojis and mix in English ("We've got you covered") to encourage.
3. **We are here for you.** We don't sell products; we sell solutions for endurance and against pain, function that makes the everyday easier, and the possibility of personal expression within healthcare.

**OUR STRATEGY IN SHORT: We don't sell things. We sell endurance (ork), pride and identity.**

## Visual branding — "Authentic joy & professional care"

Our visual language should be a splash of colour in an otherwise sterile world. We balance the playful with the competent. It should feel like a warm hug from a colleague, not a cold instruction from an authority.

1. **Reflect healthcare's reality.** Our images should represent all the heroes in the corridor — regardless of age, origin or body type. We celebrate the person behind the uniform.
2. **Colourful.** Our pink colour and our patterns are not just decoration — they are symbols of work joy and identity. In a grey/white environment, Vårdväskan should light up. We use colour to evoke emotion, but with clean typography and layout so we never lose our professionalism.
3. **Warm and playful without becoming childish.** We should be inspiring and fun, but never unserious. We sell professional equipment to highly educated care providers.

"När kunden ser oss ska de känna: Här är någon som förstår mitt jobb och vill göra det roligare." ("When the customer sees us, they should feel: here is someone who understands my job and wants to make it more fun.") (An AI version of the visual concept is also shown in the deck.)

## Marketing (B2C)

**Where should we be visible?**

Digital: Meta, Snapchat, TikTok, YouTube, influencer marketing, email marketing, SMS marketing, content marketing (blog, guides), SEO & AEO, Google Shopping & display advertising, collaborations with students, associations and organizations.

Physical: fairs, pop-up shop.

**Content mix:**

- **Content marketing:** value-creating content mixing utility with entertainment. Focus on solving the individual's everyday problems (e.g. "How to avoid back pain") and topics that create recognition around working in healthcare.
- **SEO, SEM & AEO:** optimize for purchase and problem-solving search terms and key categories; optimize for AEO going forward.
- **Social media:** build a living community on Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat. Here we are "one of them", not a supplier. Focus on Reels/video showing products in action, humorous recognition and trends.
- **Email marketing & SMS:** a good mix of campaign sends, exclusive members deals, educational and inspiring/entertaining material. Complement with SMS for extra push.
- **CRM / journeys:** automated email flows based on behaviour — cross-sell, win-back, replenishment etc.
- **Social proof:** authentic UGC and customer reviews (Trustpilot). Let ordinary nurses and micro-influencers show the products in their real environment.
- **Ambassador program:** a dedicated group of micro-influencers and healthcare profiles as the face outwards. They create a constant stream of content and credibility in their own networks, which we can share in our channels.

**How do we win the customer?** Professional products; one-stop-shop; wide assortment; community; affordable; inspiration.

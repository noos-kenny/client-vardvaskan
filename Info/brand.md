# Brand — Vårdväskan

## Brand promise
What problem do they solve?

Per the client's B2C strategy 2026: **"Vi ger din arbetsdag färg och din kropp styrkan att orka"** (We give your working day colour and your body the strength to endure). Vårdväskan solves two problems at once for care staff: (1) physical endurance — hard floors, long shifts, tired legs/feet/backs — through ergonomic shoes, compression socks and functional equipment; (2) loss of individuality in a sterile, uniform environment — through colour, patterns and personal style ("be YOU at work"). Strategy in short (per client): "We don't sell things. We sell endurance (ork), pride and identity." Core promise line: "Vi tar hand om dig, så att du orkar ta hand om andra." B2B promise (Vårdväskan Företag): "Vi gör inköpen enkla – och personalen stoltare" — simple purchasing, zero administration (Provlådan, skobidrag, företagskonto), prouder staff. (as of 2026)

## Challenges
- Content and creative production is the limiting factor for scaling the strategically most important categories — work shoes, compression socks, work accessories (NOOS claim, as of 2026).
- Vårdväskan's brand awareness has declined over time; brand search volume trend negative (56K claim, as of 2024-12; ~27,000 brand searches/month SE).
- Color4Care has very low brand awareness in the Nordics (NO 2,400, FI 2,700, DK 240 searches/month) and flat organic development; DK declined significantly (56K claim, as of 2024–2025).
- Dependence on Beez: 65% of SE sales (56K claim, as of 2024-12).
- Important USPs missing in the purchase journey (free shipping limit, returns, delivery times/options on product page and in checkout), suppressing conversion (56K claim, as of 2024-12).
- Not targeting the "herr" (men's) segment — untapped market (56K claim, as of 2024–2025).

## Brand platform
Tonality and do's and don'ts.

**Voice (B2C, per client tone-of-voice guide 2026):** warm, personal, inclusive, positive, light-hearted; the encouraging colleague in the break room; twinkle in the eye, a bit cheeky — never silly, childish, stiff, condescending or overly salesy. The care professional is the hero, not the product (don't overuse the word "hjälte"). Sell the result and the feeling (lighter steps, fresher legs, more colour on the shift), not just the product. Speak *with* healthcare, not *to* it — "hard floors", "coffee stains", "12-hour shifts". Use "du" and "vi"; short sentences; everyday language. Concept language: "be YOU at work", mix & match, good vibes, vårdlook/arbetslook. English used sparingly and playfully ("we've got you covered", "new in"). CTAs positive, no hard sales pressure ("Shoppa din stil", "Hitta din favorit" — never "Köp nu!!!"). Emojis sparingly.

**Don'ts:** corporate/bureaucratic language ("tillhandahålla", "möjliggöra", "unika erbjudanden"), dry B2B tone, too many exclamation marks, shouting caps, care clichés, cutesy/condescending tone, talking more about the brand than the care staff, desperate/aggressive urgency, **hard or medically guaranteeing health promises** (e.g. "knän som håller till pensionen").

**Taglines:** "Vårdväskan - Tillsammans sätter vi färg på vården" (short version); alternative: "För dig som står upp för andra → Tillsammans sätter vi färg på vården". B2B taglines drafted but explicitly not finalized ("ej spikat"): "Enklare inköp och stoltare vårdpersonal" / "Arbetskläder, utrustning och namnskyltar för ert team". (as of 2026)

## Position
Towards competitors.

Per client strategy 2026: the Nordics' widest assortment for care professionals; one-stop-shop ("Allt för ditt skift. Utan krångel."); community/movement rather than supplier ("Tillsammans sätter vi färg på vården"); a splash of colour in a sterile category ("Lite mindre vitt. Mycket mer du."); ergonomics + joy combined. B2C targets nurses, assistant nurses, medical secretaries, physicians, veterinarians, dentists. B2B position: the simple, admin-free workwear partner for small and medium private clinics (Provlådan, skobidrag, företagskonto) — not competing for large public tenders.

Market position (56K data, as of 2025-03): second biggest share of search in the SE category overall; biggest within "namnskyltar", second within "stödstrumpor" (after pharmacy giants Apotea/Apotek Hjärtat) and "vårdkläder", mid-market within "arbetsskor".

## Graphical guidelines
Per the visual identity manual (as of 2023-04-19) and color codes doc:

- **Vårdväskan is a pink company.** Primary pink HEX FF296B (PMS 213); darker pink B80048; lighter pink FFCCE1; complements red EA394E, purple 7056D1, blue 3C3C99; black + 75/50/30% greys; white. Contrast is the primary rule for combinations.
- **Logotype:** symbol + wordmark; used in white, primary pink or black; primary placement centered; min width 3 cm; clear-space rules based on logo height. Color4Care logotype for international markets, same rules. Symbol can stand alone on packaging/totes.
- **Typography: Graphik** (Bold, Medium, Regular); caps for punchy headlines; large headline/body size contrast.
- **Photography:** modern, pared-back, human; natural people of different genders, ages and appearances; bright, clean, professional; products on light-grey background or colorful conceptual flat-lays from above.
- **Graphic elements:** offer/news/price devices predominantly in primary pink; icons only from the icon library.
- Visual branding direction 2026 (per client B2C strategy): "Authentic joy & professional care" — reflect healthcare's reality (all heroes in the corridor, regardless of age, origin, body type); colourful but with clean typography; warm and playful without becoming childish.
- Assets: logo packages (PNG/SVG/AI, SoMe, Beez) and Graphik font files exist in the client's Drive brandbook folder. Full manual: `Upload/260821-brand-manual-2023.md`; colors: `Upload/260821-brand-color-codes.md`.

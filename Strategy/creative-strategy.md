## Audience / Personas
Triggers etc. Includes relevant trends and subcultures in the client's market.

Client-defined B2C personas (per client B2C strategy 2026):

- **"Fräcka Felicia"** — 26, assistant nurse (undersköterska) at Södersjukhuset, apartment in a Stockholm suburb. Channels: TikTok, Snapchat, YouTube. Inspired by Bianca Ingrosso, Johanna & Edwin; watches Young Royals, Unga mammor; gym in aim'n/Stronger; shops H&M, Gina, Caia, Shein. Likes glitter & gold, pink & purple, floral. - **"Rutinerade Rita"** — 55, nurse (sjuksköterska) in home care, terraced house in a smaller town, husband, daughter also in healthcare. Channels: Facebook, Instagram. Shops Gekås, Ellos, KappAhl; reads Femina, Damernas Värld; likes Mello, flowers & animals, Pernilla Wahlgren. Values practicality, comfort, trusted brands (or cheeky and cheap), no-hassle shopping, deals/price. Need-driven purchases when things wear out — replenishment reminders work; strongly influenced by colleagues' choices. Content: guides (how to create the look), product tips, educational content.

B2C audience: nurses, assistant nurses, medical secretaries, physicians, veterinarians, dentists and more. B2B audiences: purchasers, medical secretaries, nurses at small/medium clinics; HR/operations managers/purchasers on LinkedIn. Emotional triggers (per client): make the working day easier, personal expression, professional pride, empowerment, belonging, simple consolidated purchases.

## Core segments & barriers
1–3 behaviour-based core segments (situation and motive, not age and income), with sub-segments. Per segment: what drives them, and what stops them. Barriers are recorded in two layers — what the client *assumes* stops people, and what customers actually say (see Info/customer-voice.md). The gap between the layers is where the insight sits.

Partially collected (customer-base facts, per client B2C strategy 2026): >90% of customers are women; nurses + assistant nurses >85% of B2C customers; physicians just under 10%. SE addressable audience ~530,000 care professionals (427,000 women); NO ~360k, DK ~120k, FI ~358k. 293,000 unique customers last 24 months. Minor category variation within segments (assistant nurses don't buy equipment); black/navy/white dominate all markets. Compression socks skew to returning customers; Littmann is a one-time purchase. Behaviour-based segmentation with barriers per segment: *Not yet collected* (planned as part of NOOS Commercial Insights workstream — purchase journeys, triggers, motivations and barriers per category, per NOOS proposal 2026).

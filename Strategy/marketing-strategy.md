## Historical performance & learnings
What has worked, what hasn't. Includes known bottlenecks and learnings from previous partners.

From 56K's account analyses (agency claims based on account data, as of 2024-12 unless noted):

- **Google Ads:** 60% of revenue from PMax/Shopping vs healthy benchmark ~80%; reallocation to 70/30 estimated +4.7 MSEK revenue. 1,240 of 12,407 SKUs = 91% of shopping revenue; 45% of SKUs got no traffic Jan–Nov 2024; 82% of item IDs <10 clicks/month. 42% of shopping spend on overbid products. 75% of product titles <50 chars, hurting visibility/CTR. 46% of search budget in 270 ad groups with COS >14% (avg 25%); ad-group cannibalization on key terms (e.g. "stetoskop" split across 5 ad groups). Audience gaps: customer match in 1/18 campaigns, custom intent 0/18; in-market "Socks" audience 115x more likely to convert. Demand Gen + YouTube untapped.
- **Meta:** mainly prospecting, no CRM/cross-sell campaigns, broad remarketing; creatives criticized — small unreadable fonts, fake "se mer" buttons with mismatched CTA, message not clear within 2 seconds; estimated +20% CTR (~105k extra visitors/year) from strategic creative work. No upsell strategy.
- **SEO (website audit, as of 2025-03):** health score 43% (55/102 checks failed, 16 high priority); <50% of pages indexed; B2B site canonicalized to B2C site and noindexed; thousands of broken links/redirect chains; JS-rendered main menu; 57% of pages get no organic clicks; strong rankings on core terms (stödstrumpor #2, namnskyltar #2, stetoskop #2, otoskop #1); "herr" segment untargeted (~0.6% of available men/women traffic captured); big faceted-navigation/subcategory expansion potential (birkenstock dam 22,200/m etc.); accessibility 71% vs WCAG 2.1 AA; Core Web Vitals failing (LCP, CLS, INP).
- **Brand:** brand search volume in negative trend; Color4Care awareness very low (56K, 2024-12).
- **CRM (per client, 2026):** scale exists (22.3M emails/year B2C SE); learning focus is precision (engagement/intent) over volume; RFM/churn-risk analysis limited by Mailchimp functionality.
